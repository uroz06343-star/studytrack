Questions

Do I need an account?

No. The free planner works without one, and your list is saved in your browser.

What happens to my data?

Your list and notes stay on your device. Text is sent to an AI provider only when you use Study Lab. Pro sync, when it launches, will store your list on our servers so it can reach your other devices. Read the privacy summary.

Can I use it if I'm under 13?

Only with a parent or guardian's permission. Please don't enter personal details in your notes.

Is Study Lab allowed at my school?

That depends on your school. Check your academic-integrity rules before using AI help on graded work.

What if I clear my browser data?

On the free plan your list is deleted with it. Pro sync is designed to prevent this.
